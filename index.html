Gates computed on QQQ daily closes: 20-day realised vol < 35%, 20d/250d vol ratio < 1.10, drawdown from the 250-day high better than −20% (re-entry above −10%), all three holding two consecutive sessions. Prices are refreshed once each weekday after the close and served from this site, so there is no API key and nothing to configure. The tested rule uses the Nasdaq-100 index and total returns; QQQ price tracks it closely but is not identical, so this is the field version of tqqq-strategy/spec.py, not a replacement for it. Reads the prior close — act at the next one. Not investment advice.
